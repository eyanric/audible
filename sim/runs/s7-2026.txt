== P1: what the FFA weekly board will score in 2026 ==
A PREDICTION INTERVAL, NOT A CONFIDENCE INTERVAL, and the first version of this file
shipped the wrong one. `mean +- 2*sd` covers a NEW draw only about 89% of the time at
n = 7, because it ignores both the uncertainty in the mean and the t-distribution's
tails. The 95% interval for one future season is mean +- t(6, .975) * s * sqrt(1 +
1/7) = mean +- 2.447 * s * 1.0690, which is 1.31x wider.
REFUTED if the 2026 mean over weeks 1-17 falls outside it, vorp scale, symmetric.

A FLAT PREDICTION CANNOT DISCRIMINATE A TREND, so the trend is stated beside it. All
three leagues decline across 2019-2025 and the OLS extrapolation for 2026 sits INSIDE
the flat interval, so P1 holding is consistent with both models. The second line is

== P2: no term re-tested in 2026 will resolve in more than one league ==
Phases 2 and 3 ran 24 terms in 3 leagues over 7 seasons. The prediction is that a
2026 re-run of the same 24 terms produces at most ONE term with RESOLVES in two or
more leagues. REFUTED by two or more such terms.

P2 IS THE WEAKEST PREDICTION HERE AND IS LABELLED AS SUCH. At the observed base rate
of resolutions, the chance that two or more terms replicate under a pure null is
roughly 13%, so P2 confirms about seven times in eight whether or not anything is
real. It is kept because a refutation would be informative and because removing a
weak prediction after writing it is worse than labelling it.
